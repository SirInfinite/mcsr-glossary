# MCSR Glossary Content Sources

Research reviewed: 2026-09-02

This file records the evidence used for the v1 corrections and additions in `data/terms.json`. Current rules and Ranked behavior were checked against their official documentation. Minecraft mechanics were checked against Minecraft Wiki, while community technique names and 1.16.1 routing were checked against the official MCSR resource index, primary tool documentation, and the established Metacor guide.

Definitions deliberately avoid historical creation dates when no authoritative date was found. Empty date fields mean “not established,” not “created at launch.” The controlled categories used for v1 are `format`, `strategy`, `technique`, `terminology`, and `tool`.

## Human Review Notes

- `Classic` and `Hypermodern` have stable meaning in the cited Ranked/Java 1.16.1 context, but runners sometimes use the labels more loosely outside Ranked. Their definitions are intentionally scoped rather than presented as universal taxonomy.
- `SSG` commonly means Set Seed Glitchless on the Minecraft Any% board. It is retained as a search alias for Set Seed, with the distinction stated in the definition.
- Community spellings for `Pie-Ray`, `Piedar`, `E Travel`, and `Calc Travel` vary. The aliases included here are supported by the cited guide/resource vocabulary, but community editors may prefer a different house style.
- Historical creation dates remain empty because the available sources establish usage and mechanics, not reliable invention dates.
