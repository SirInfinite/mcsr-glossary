About MCSR Glossary

Why it exists

A clearer entrance to a technical community

Minecraft speedrunning has specialized language for routes, structures, tools, timing, and competitive play. MCSR Glossary turns that vocabulary into a searchable reference for runners and viewers who are still learning the scene.

Built for the run

Fast to scan, easy to share

Stable term links, compact metadata, related concepts, and inline demonstrations keep each entry useful during practice, commentary, or a quick rules check.

Open source

Inspect it, question it, improve it

The frontend, validation rules, migrations, source notes, and QA reports are public. Corrections are welcome—especially when terminology changes or a definition needs better version context.
