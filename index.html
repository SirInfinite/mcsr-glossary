    About the glossary

    Minecraft speedrunning has its own language. This is a place to look it up.

    For runners & viewers

    MCSR Glossary explains the routes, techniques, formats, tools, and shorthand used in Minecraft speedrunning. Search an unfamiliar term, follow a related concept, or share a definition during practice.

    Researched in context

    Definitions are checked against community guides, category rules, tool documentation, and technical references. Source notes record the evidence for each term. Historical and legacy entries keep older runs and guides understandable.

    Read the source notes ↗

    Help keep it accurate

    Suggest a missing term, propose an edit, or report a problem from a definition. Maintainers review contributions before publication. The project is open source and in early beta; wording and coverage are still being refined.
